How to clear it

A 20-game streak is about consistency rather than peak talent — avoid a roster with one superstar and four weak links, since the sim punishes thin rosters over long stretches.

Every challenge is scored on the season, not the draft — finishing the roster is not a pass on its own. You get one attempt per day, and the board resets at midnight UTC.
