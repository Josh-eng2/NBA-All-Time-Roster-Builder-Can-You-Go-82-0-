👎 Boos Only

Draft constraint — a rule limiting who you may draft, enforced at pick time.

Total roster fans under 300 — win 50+ games.

▶ Play today's challenge

What the rule means

Fans budget
300 total across all five starters (average 60 each; the cheapest player in the game is 35)
Win floor
50 of 82 games

How to clear it

This is the hardest constraint in the catalog, because fans and quality correlate. Spend most of the budget on one genuinely good starter and fill the other four from the cheapest end of the board. Chemistry matters more here than anywhere else: a cheap roster that fits together beats a cheap roster of mismatched parts.

Every challenge is scored on the season, not the draft — finishing the roster is not a pass on its own. You get one attempt per day, and the board resets at midnight UTC.

When Boos Only has run

This one has not come up yet since the Daily Challenge launched on July 14, 2026. The rotation is seeded by date, so it will.
